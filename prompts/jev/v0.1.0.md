Question sets asked of TypeSafe Jev (System One). Each set is asked over a named-field JSON state, all questions of a
set together, one narrow judgment per question, every Choice with a no-match option.

- `catalyst.v1`: is_material (Noul), direction (Choice: bullish | bearish | mixed | none), kind (Choice: earnings |
  guidance | analyst | m_and_a | regulatory_legal | product | financing | management | macro | other | none),
  time_horizon (Score: intraday | days | weeks).
- `triage.v1` (Slice 5): setup_quality, catalyst_strength, chase_risk (Scores), skip (Noul).
- `trigger.v1` (Slice 6): invalidates_thesis (Noul).
